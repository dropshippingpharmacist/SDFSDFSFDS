# Rejection block tests, 2026-11-12

Owner: "test them", the four changes that would have found Mountain Man's PEPE short.

## What was tested

Every tap-2 sweep rejection block the detector finds (the PEPE type: a deviation beyond a confirmed range extreme that
leaves a wick, price back inside), wick >= 35%, on 30 markets, 1h / 2h / 4h / 6h, about 2,400 bars each: **5,457 blocks**
(3,370 with a wick of 50% or more). Each is traded as a resting limit, stop beyond the wick tip (the bot's own stop),
target the opposite range extreme, 3R floor, the live exit (70% off at 1R, the rest at the target, stop never moved),
fees included, stop first when one bar touches both.

These are RAW blocks: no grade-A checklist, no higher-timeframe zone, no SMT. Third-tap and fourth-tap blocks were not
in this test.

## Results

|---|---|---|---|---|
| start of the wick | 2,503 | −0.08R | 46% | 23% |
| 0.5 | 2,666 | −0.13R | 44% | 18% |
| 0.618 | 2,610 | −0.16R | 42% | 17% |
| 0.705 | 2,562 | −0.21R | 40% | 15% |
| 0.786 | 2,525 | −0.25R | 39% | 15% |

The deeper the limit, the worse. The 0.618 limit switched on earlier the same day from one replayed trade (PEPE) was
wrong and is switched off again (`RB_ENTRY_FIB=0`).

**1. A lighter wick** (limit 0.618): wick >= 50% −0.16R (2,610 trades); 40–50% −0.20R (1,119); 35–40% −0.14R (538). No
gain from loosening the 50% rule. Not changed.

**2. A longer life** (limit 0.618): 24 bars −0.17R; 48 bars −0.16R; 96, 144, 240 bars −0.16R. The 49 trades that only
exist with a life past 48 bars averaged +0.09R. No real difference. Not changed.

**4. Letting a block compete with other setups** adds this same population of trades, which loses on average. Not
changed.

**Other splits** (limit at the start of the wick): by timeframe 1h −0.25R, 2h −0.09R, 4h −0.19R, 6h −0.13R (at the
0.618); shorts −0.11R, longs −0.20R; a sweep deeper than 30% of the range −0.30R.

## What else it showed

On a sweep block (drawn from the range line to the sweep tip) the bot's "fib 0.705–0.786" reason was true on **all 3,370**
blocks and its "multi-timeframe" reason on **3,029**. So the two-reasons rule added that day filtered nothing on these
blocks. Fixed: on a sweep block those two are no longer counted; it needs two of SMT, higher-timeframe block, USDT.D
mirror, lower-timeframe break of structure.

## Reading

A tap-2 sweep rejection block on its own has no edge in this data: every variant loses a little. That agrees with the
sources ("reacting of nothing but RBs … tends to be just temporary reactions"; "you cannot get a higher probability RB"
only with the multi-timeframe block, the fib, the quality zone and the opposite draw together). Whatever edge these
have has to come from the filters around the block, and those were not measurable here: blocks that pass the full
grade-A gate are too rare to count.
