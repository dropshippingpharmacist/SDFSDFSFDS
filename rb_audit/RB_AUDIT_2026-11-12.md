## Correction, added later on 2026-11-12

This audit missed a source. An earlier study of Lars's rejection-block videos exists, numbered RBS1 to at least RBS42
("REJECTION_BLOCK_STUDY", with video timestamps such as "L21 23:54" and "v4 13:43"). Its rules are cited throughout the
code and in `FINAL_SWEEP_2026-11-09.md`, but the study file itself was never committed to this repository, so it could
not be re-read here and the table above was not checked against it rule by rule. The owner was right that those
streams had been studied.

What the repository does record about that study:

- **Coded from it:** RBS30 (the block drawn from the range line to the sweep tip), RBS27 (the two-tone midpoint),
  RBS4 / RBS23 / RBS24 / RBS28 / RBS33 (the lower-timeframe reconfirmation after a front-run).
- **Listed as still not coded** (`FINAL_SWEEP_2026-11-09.md`): RBS8, RBS10, RBS15, RBS26, RBS31, RBS32, RBS35, RBS36,
  RBS37. Described there: a partial take-profit at the opposing block with a runner to the highs (RBS15); a block at
  the range high after the range low was taken, or inside a compression, gives a temporary reaction only (RBS10 /
  RBS32); the daily → weekly → local cascade and the two-stage position with a lower-timeframe add (RBS2 / RBS14 /
  RBS35); fib-at-edge and blind-limit points (RBS36 and others).

Decision recorded the same day: the reconfirmation trigger keeps its tolerance at the block's edge. On PEPE (10 Nov)
it fired on two 15m highs just under the block, before price had entered it. Requiring the highs to be inside the
block was considered and rejected, because RBS23 / RBS24 / RBS28 define this entry as the one taken after a FRONT-RUN:
the block is tapped "at or just above the edge" and the resting limit is missed (LINK 10.63 against a 10.606 limit).
A bottom that forms just outside the block is the case the rule exists for.

To close this gap the study file is needed: the owner's copy, or the videos again.
