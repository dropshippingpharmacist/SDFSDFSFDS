## Swing fib built (later the same day)

RBS18 / RBS23 / RBS26 are now coded. `_rb_fib_entry` pulls the fib from tap 2 (1.0) to the swing extreme printed after
it (0.0); for a tap-2 sweep block tap 2 is the block's own tip and the swing is the bounce since, for a tap-3 block it
is tap 2 → the extreme between the taps. `swing_edge` is true when the block's near edge sits at 0.705–0.786 of that
swing, and that counts as one of the reasons a block needs before the break.

Why that band: on the 3,370 heavy sweep blocks, blocks whose edge sat at 0.705–0.786 when price came back were the
only group that did not lose (171 trades, 49% wins, +0.01R per trade). Edge at 0.618–0.705: −0.18R. Edge at
0.786–0.9: −0.11R. All blocks: −0.12R. So the band is where the sources say it is, and it is break-even, not
profitable, on its own.

Limits: the read moves while the bounce is still growing, so it can turn true and later pass out of the band; the
PEPE 10 Nov block read about 0.46 when price came back, so it does not qualify.

## RBS36 built (later the same day)

No blind limit far below the 0.786: before the break, a block whose edge sits deeper than 0.85 on the swing fib is
refused as a resting limit (`RB_BLIND_LIMIT_MAX_RETR`); the lower-timeframe confirmation entry is exempt, and so is the
block after the confirmed break. "Way below the 786" is his wording, so the line is set a little past the 0.786, not on
it. An order that is already resting is not cancelled if the swing later grows past the line.
