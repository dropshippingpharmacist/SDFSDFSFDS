The lore

You have a friend from school on Facebook. You live twenty minutes apart. You haven't seen each other in four years and you probably never will, because nobody texts "hey, I'll be at the taco spot on 5th at 7."

Route does it for you. Add each other, share where you're heading, and when your paths cross, Route tells you both. One tap and you pull up. That's it. That's the whole idea: the people already around you, actually around you.

How Route works

Add each otherBoth of you add each other by username. Until you both do, neither sees a thing. No strangers, ever.
Share where you're heading"Taco spot on 5th, leaving in 30." Friends see the area, never your doorstep, and it disappears on its own.
Cross pathsHeading near the same place at the same time? Route flags it: crossing paths.
Pull upTap Pull up. They say "I'm down" or "not now". Ghost mode hides you from everyone in one tap.

The game: link-ups

When you pull up and actually meet, you both tap "We linked up". Both confirm, you both score.

10 pointsEvery confirmed link-up. One per friend per day counts, so it rewards seeing people, not farming.
+25 ReconnectedThe first link-up ever with a friend. The friend you haven't seen in years is worth the most.
Streaks and badgesLink up every week to keep your streak. Badges: First Route, Pulled Up, Linked, Social Butterfly, Regular.
Friends leaderboardYou against your own friends. Points live in the app; they are not tokens and pay nothing.

FAQ

Is Route live?

Yes. Open the app, make an account, and add a friend by username.

Do I need $ROUTE to use the app?

No. The app is free and works without the coin.

Does holding $ROUTE earn points or rewards?

No. Points come only from real link-ups with friends, and they stay in the app.

Can strangers see where I am?

No. Only people you have both added see anything, and only an area, for a limited time.

What is the contract address?

The one at the top of this page. Always check it here before buying anything named Route.

Is this financial advice?

No. Memecoins can go to zero in minutes. Only spend what you can afford to lose.
