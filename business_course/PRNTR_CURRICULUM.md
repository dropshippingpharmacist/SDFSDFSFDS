**One path:** make money on memecoins → keep it → grow it with leverage and higher-timeframe positions → use the
higher timeframe to find the next memecoins early. Nobody else teaches the full loop; that loop is the product.

Education only, not financial advice. Most memecoins go to zero and most leveraged traders lose money. No lesson,
tool or call promises an income; past results, including the owner's, do not guarantee future results.

Existing material this curriculum uses: `memecoin_course/PRNTR_Memecoins.pdf` (12 lessons),
`crossover/PRNTR_Crossover_and_Crypto_Income.pdf` (Parts A–E, 13 case studies), `Trading_Mentorship_Playbook.pdf`,

## The planner (Module 0.4)

Inside the course only, after purchase. Never in ads, DMs, the site or on sales calls (that would be an earnings
claim).

**They enter:** starting capital, the part they can afford to lose, risk per trade (default 1%), the split from
Module 2, trades per week they can realistically take.

**It shows:**
1. **Risk math:** dollars at risk per trade, position size and liquidation distance at a given leverage, how many
   losses in a row before the account is down 10% / 20%.
2. **Simulated ranges:** thousands of simulated sequences drawn from the system's past replay stats (win rate, R
   per win, −1R per loss), showing the worst 10%, the middle and the best 10%, **plus the probability of a losing
   year** and the deepest drawdown. Labelled "a simulation of past statistics, not a promise; real results include
   fees, slippage, missed trades and mistakes."
3. **What breaks it:** the same simulation with 3% risk, and with revenge trades, so they see why the rules exist.

Memecoin outcomes are not simulated: too random to model honestly. The planner covers the leverage lane and the
split.
