# The PRNTR Curriculum

**One path, the Print Loop:** make money on memecoins → keep it → grow it with leverage and Big Chart positions →
use the Big Chart to find the next memecoins early. Every word used is in `PRNTR_GLOSSARY.md`; students learn the
method in those words only.

Education only, not financial advice. Most memecoins go to zero and most leveraged traders lose money. No lesson,
tool or call promises an income; past results, including the owner's, do not guarantee future results.

Material this curriculum uses: `memecoin_course/PRNTR_Memecoins.pdf` (12 lessons),
`crossover/PRNTR_Crossover_and_Crypto_Income.pdf` (Parts A–E, 13 case studies), the PRNTR Method workbook
(`prntr_course/`, Part 1 done), the PRNTR bot (signals, alert cards, the Scorecard), `legal/`.

## The planner (Module 0.4)

Inside the course only, after purchase. Never in ads, DMs, the site or on sales calls (that would be an earnings
claim).

**They enter:** starting money, the part they can afford to lose, risk per trade (default 1%), the split from
Module 2, the trades per week they can realistically take.

**It shows:**
1. **Risk math:** dollars at risk per trade, position size and liquidation distance at a given leverage, how many
   losses in a row before the account is down 10% / 20%.
2. **Simulated ranges:** thousands of simulated sequences drawn from the system's past test stats (win rate, R per
   win, −1R per loss): the worst 10%, the middle and the best 10%, **plus the chance of a losing year** and the
   deepest drop. Labelled "a simulation of past statistics, not a promise; real results include fees, slippage,
   missed trades and mistakes."
3. **What breaks it:** the same simulation at 3% risk, and with revenge trades, so they see why the rules exist.

Memecoin outcomes are not simulated: too random to model honestly. The planner covers the leverage lane and the
split.
