# The PRNTR Language

Every word the course, the cards, the videos and the calls use. Students learn the method in these words only.
It builds on the words already in the PRNTR Method workbook (the Box, the Fake-Out, the Return, the Turn, the
Launch Pad) and never contradicts them. Education only, not financial advice.

## Reading the chart (the Russian dolls)
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Big Chart** | The chart that tells the story: 4-hour up to monthly. Always read first |
| **Middle Chart** | The chart where the setup forms |
| **Small Chart** | The chart you enter on |
| **The Russian dolls** | Each chart sits inside the bigger one; a small chart never beats what the big chart says |
| **A Swing** | A real high or low: price turned there and moved away by more than a normal candle |
| **Main Swings / Inner Swings** | The swings that define the trend / the small ones inside them. Only Main Swings count for the Turn |
| **A Step** | Price breaks a Main Swing in the direction it's already going: the trend continues |
| **The Turn** | Price breaks the last Main Swing *against* the trend: the first sign the story has changed |
| **The Turn Ladder** | Checking several Small Charts for the Turn: the bigger the zone, the bigger the chart the Turn must show on |

## The Box
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Box** | Where price rests: a ceiling, a floor and the middle |
| **Touches** | Each test of the ceiling or floor. More touches, stronger Box |
| **Cheap half / Expensive half** | Below / above the middle. Buy cheap, sell expensive, never trade the middle |
| **The Edge** | The outer quarter of the Box on your side. PRNTR Prime trades only start here |
| **Quarter Line / Half Line** | How a Box dies: a close past the Quarter Line is a warning, past the Half Line the Box is over |
| **The trip across** | After the Return, the move to the other side of the Box: the first target |
| **Running / Resting** | The market trending vs boxing. Know which one you're in before every trade |

## The Fake-Out and the Launch Pad
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Fake-Out** | Price pokes outside the Box, grabs the stops, closes back in |
| **The Fake-Out limit** | How far a Fake-Out may go: if candles close beyond it and keep going, it's a real breakout and there's no trade |
| **The Return** | The close back inside the Box. No Return, no trade |
| **Base / Roof** | A Box at the bottom of a move where buyers build / at the top where sellers build |
| **The Final Fake-Out** | The last, deepest Fake-Out of a Base or Roof, the one that traps the most people |
| **The Launch Pad** | The first higher low after the Final Fake-Out (lower high, for shorts): where the trade is taken |
| **Setup A / Setup B** | The two PRNTR Setups: A launches straight from the Final Fake-Out, B launches from a later Touch |
| **Two-Doll / Three-Doll Setup** | A whole Box-Fake-Out-Return forming *inside* the Fake-Out of a bigger one, two or three charts deep |

## Zones (where the big orders sit)
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Vault** | The PRNTR core zone: one Big Chart candle locked behind price gaps. Price comes back to it |
| **Locks (1–8)** | How many gaps lock a Vault. More locks, stronger Vault |
| **Origin Zone** | The last candle before a strong move started |
| **Flip Zone** | A zone that broke and now works from the other side |
| **Gap** | A stretch price skipped through with no trading back and forth |
| **Open Level** | A candle's open price the market hasn't come back to |
| **Stack** | Zones from several charts sitting on top of each other |
| **Fresh** | A zone price hasn't touched since it formed |
| **The Zone Test** | Six checks: it caused a Step or a Turn · it's a Flip Zone · it grabbed stops · heavy volume inside · Bait before it · formed inside a Base or Roof |

## Traps (who gets caught)
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Stop Piles** | The orders resting just outside every Box, high and low |
| **Stop Grab** | Price runs a Stop Pile and comes back |
| **Bait** | An obvious small level that pulls traders in early, before the real move |
| **Wipeout Zones** | Prices where leveraged traders get force-closed (the blue bands on PRNTR cards) |
| **Hidden Walls** | Big orders that keep refilling, and order-book walls that don't move |
| **Clear Road / Roadblock** | Nothing / something in the way of the target |

## The trigger (when to get in)
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Snapback** | A 3-candle reversal at a zone: candle 1 grabs the low, candle 2 holds, candle 3 closes back strong |
| **The Shove** | A Turn with force: big bodies, volume, follow-through, a Gap left behind |
| **No Grind** | The change comes fast. A slow drift into the zone is a warning |
| **The Split** | Two related markets disagree at the zone (one makes a new low, the other doesn't) |
| **The Print Pocket** | 0.618–0.786 of the Turn's move: where the entry order waits |
| **Overshoot Levels** | Targets beyond the swing (−0.5, −0.618, −1 of it) |
| **Line in the Sand** | The low (or high) the trade depends on. The stop goes beyond it |
| **The Busy Price** | The price with the most volume traded in the Box |
| **Value Ceiling / Value Floor** | The edges of where most of the volume traded |

## The rest of the market (the Gauges)
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fear Gauge** | USDT dominance: rising = money hiding in stablecoins, falling = money taking risk |
| **Rotation Gauges** | BTC dominance, ETH/BTC, TOTAL3: where the money is moving |
| **Ratio Charts** | A coin priced in another coin (coin/BTC, coin/SOL) to see its real strength |

## The trades
| PRNTR term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Scorecard** | The PRNTR Prime checklist (Module 8 of the Method) |
| **PRNTR Prime** | Passes the whole Scorecard: a Vault + a Snapback on the Big Chart + at the Edge, not the middle + a stop outside the normal noise + the Split, then the Turn. Only on markets where the tests prove it |
| **PRNTR Setup** | A valid trade that misses part of the Scorecard: smaller size, and the alert says what's missing |
| **Quick Print / Day Print / Prime Print** | A scalp / a day trade / a big-chart swing: three intentions, three sizes, three ways to manage |
| **The Runner** | The part of a position left on for the bigger target |
| **The Print Loop** | Make (memecoins) → Keep (pay yourself) → Grow (leverage and big-chart positions) → the Crossover finds the next coins |
| **The PRNTR Crossover** | Stock stories and the Big Chart → the memecoin wave the story creates |

## Only PRNTR words
Slides, cards, videos and calls use these words and no other system's. If a student asks "isn't this the same as
X?", the answer is: "PRNTR is our own system. The ideas behind price action are public; the rules, the Scorecard
and the tests behind them are ours."
