# The close: what to say and do in the last 10 minutes of the call

Use after discovery and the pitch (blueprint section 6). The point is a clean, recorded, signed sale: the buyer knows
exactly what they're getting and has said so out loud. That is your best defence against a chargeback.

## Before the call
- Fathom (or Zoom recording) is **on**. Say at the start: "I record these so you can rewatch it. That OK?" Wait for "yes".
- Have the agreement ready in PandaDoc or DocuSign with their name and email filled in.
- Have the three checkout links open (pay in full / 2-pay / 3-pay, in `business_course/whop/WHOP_SETUP.md`).

## 1. The offer (say it plainly)
"Here's exactly what you get: 90 days with me. A call every two weeks, six in total. Chat with me in between. The full
course. All the signals for the 90 days. The dashboard. It's $3,000, or two payments of $1,700, or three of $1,200.
Which works for you?"

## 2. The three confirmations (on the recording, word for word, wait for each "yes")
1. "Just so we're clear: **I can't promise you'll make money.** Nobody honest can. Trading is risky and some trades lose.
   What I promise is the coaching, the calls and the signals. You're OK with that?"
2. "If in the first **14 days** it isn't what I just described, you get a full refund. After that there are no refunds.
   Fair?"
3. (Payment plan only) "The plan is the **full program price split up**, not a monthly thing you can stop. You're
   committing to all [two / three] payments. Good?"

## 3. Sign, then pay (in this order, while you're both still on the call)
1. "I'm sending you a short agreement now. It says exactly what we just went through." Send it, and wait while they
   sign. Don't send the payment link until it's signed.
2. Send **one** checkout link, the plan they chose. Stay on while they pay. Whop shows the charge as WHOP\*PRNTR; tell
   them: "It'll show on your statement as WHOP-star-PRNTR."
3. "You'll get the Discord role in a minute and I'll book our first call within 7 days."

## 4. Straight after
- Save to their row in the tracking sheet: call date, the recording link, signed agreement, plan, payment date.
- Give the Signals Member role in Discord if Whop hasn't already, and book call 1.
- Send the welcome message the same day. People who feel looked after don't dispute.

## Never say
- "You'll make your money back", "guaranteed", "easy", "everyone makes money", a win rate you can't show on the
  public track record.
- Anything about the price being "only today" unless it's true.

## If they hesitate
- Can't afford it: "No problem. The signals start at $9.99 a day if you want to see how it works first." (Start Here page.)
- Not sure: "Take the day. I'll send you the agreement so you can read it properly." Never push someone into paying on
  the call: a pressured buyer is the most likely to dispute.
