Payments are processed by Whop and appear on bank statements as **WHOP\*PRNTR**.

## PRNTR Signals subscriptions (weekly, monthly, every 3 months)

- **Cancel any time** in your Whop account or by messaging us.
- After cancelling you **keep access until the end of the period already paid** and are **not charged again**.
- Cancelling does not refund the current period, because access to that period was already delivered.
- The 1-day pass and the lifetime plan are single payments and do not renew.

## PRNTR 1-on-1 Mentorship payment plans (2 × $1,700 or 3 × $1,200)

- A payment plan spreads the **full program price**; it is not a subscription that can be cancelled to stop payments.
- Within 14 days of the first payment the mentorship can be cancelled for a full refund if it is not what was described (see the Refund Policy). After 14 days the remaining installments stay due.
- If a scheduled installment fails, access pauses until it is paid.

## Removal for misuse

Sharing logins, reposting signals or copying course material outside PRNTR ends access without a refund.
