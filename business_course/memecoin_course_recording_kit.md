# PRNTR Memecoin Course: recording kit

You don't read a script. For each lesson, answer the questions out loud as if a student asked you, and show your screen where it says **SHOW**. Talk the way you'd explain it to a friend.

**How long:** 8–15 minutes per lesson. It's fine to ramble; the editor and I cut it down.

**How to record:**
- Loom or OBS for the screen lessons, so your face sits in a corner.
- Your phone for the face-only parts.
- One file per lesson, named like `M3_finding_wallets.mp4`.

**Upload** each file here when it's done. I'll send back the transcript, the lesson notes and the slides.

## Never show on screen

- Seed phrases, private keys, or anything in a wallet's "export" or "backup" screen.
- 2FA codes, exchange API keys, or emails with login links.
- Your full main wallet address, if you don't want people following it. Blur it, or use a demo wallet.
- Real-time trades you don't want copied before you're out of them.

## Lesson 0 · Welcome and my story (face, 5–8 min)

1. Who are you, and what were you doing before crypto?
2. How did you find memecoins, and what was your first trade?
3. Walk through the run from $7K to $1.1M: the 3–4 moments that mattered most.
4. What almost went wrong, and what did it teach you?
5. Why copy trading? What made it work better than picking coins yourself?
6. What will someone be able to do after this course?
7. One honest warning you'd give a beginner before they start.

**SHOW:** your proof screenshots from the run.

## Lesson 1 · Wallets, security and avoiding scams (screen)

1. Which wallet do you use for memecoins, and why a separate one from your main money?
2. How do you set up a "burner" or trading wallet? Walk through it.
3. What scams have you seen: fake tokens, drainer links, fake airdrops, honeypots?
4. How do you check a token isn't a scam before you buy? What do you look at first?
5. What are your rules for approvals and connecting to sites?
6. What would you do in the first five minutes if a wallet got drained?

**SHOW:** setting up a fresh wallet (no seed phrase on screen), and checking a token contract.

## Lesson 2 · Where memecoin money is really made, and lost

1. How does a memecoin go from launch to 10x to zero? Draw or describe the life cycle.
2. Who makes the money: creators, early wallets, influencers, late buyers?
3. Where do most people lose money?
4. How much of your trading money goes into memecoins at most, and why?
5. What's a realistic expectation for a beginner in their first month?

**SHOW:** one coin's chart from launch to peak to now, explaining each stage.

## Lesson 2b · Front-running the calendar (screen)

1. Why are dated events (elections, the Super Bowl, the World Cup) the easiest narratives to front-run?
2. Walk through one event coin you caught: when you bought, the market cap, your % of supply, when you sold.
3. How do you build your list of names to search for an event?
4. Show how you search a name on Pump.fun and on FOMO and find the first one (the OG).
5. How do you check it's really the OG and not a copy or a scam?
6. How much do you put into each event coin, and when do you sell?
7. How do you find traders who already do this, and what do you look for in their holdings?

**SHOW:** searching an upcoming event's names on Pump.fun and FOMO, sorting by oldest, and checking the OG on a Solana explorer.

## Lesson 3 · Finding wallets that keep winning (screen)

1. What does a "winning wallet" mean to you? What numbers do you look at?
2. Which tools or sites do you use to find wallets? Walk through one search.

## Lesson 4 · Checking a wallet before you copy it (screen)

1. What's your checklist before you trust a wallet? Go point by point.
1b. On FOMO, show a trader with a consistent pattern (one buy, several sells, repeated) and one with a scattered pattern. Why do you only copy the first?
1c. How do you read their current holdings: low market cap, a big share of supply, a narrative that fits?
2. How far back do you look at its history?
3. What red flags make you drop a wallet straight away?
4. How do you check whether it sells well, not only buys well?
5. When do you stop following a wallet?

**SHOW:** running your checklist on one wallet live.

## Lesson 5 · Copy trading in practice: tools, speed and size (screen)

1. What tool do you use to copy, and how is it set up? Walk through the settings.
2. Do you copy automatically or decide each one by hand? Why?
3. How do you size each copy trade? A fixed amount, or a percentage?
4. What about slippage, fees and speed? What settings matter?
5. What happens when the wallet you copy sells? Do you sell with it?
6. Walk through one real copy trade from entry to exit.

**SHOW:** the copy-trading setup and one full trade.

## Lesson 6 · Spotting a coin while the hype is still building

2. Where do you watch: X, Telegram, on-chain data, volume?
3. How do you tell real momentum from a coordinated pump?

**SHOW:** the signals you watch on a live or recent coin.

**SHOW:** your profit-taking on a real trade.

## Lesson 8 · Moving profits out: the money loop

1. What happens to memecoin profits after you take them?
2. How much goes into your leverage account, how much into the safe stack, and how much stays for memecoins?
3. How do you bridge or move funds between chains and exchanges? Walk through it.
4. How did this loop let you live off trading full time?
5. How do you pay yourself each month?
6. What's the one rule that keeps the loop from collapsing? (For example: nothing goes back in to win back a loss.)

**SHOW:** moving funds from a memecoin wallet to an exchange (small amount, no keys on screen).
