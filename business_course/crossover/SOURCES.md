# PRNTR Crossover and Crypto Income: sources

Research current to 14 Nov 2026. These are the links behind the figures in `PRNTR_Crossover_and_Crypto_Income.pdf`.

**How the figures were made**
- **Stock charts:** drawn from Yahoo Finance daily data in `data/` by `build_cases.py`. The Box, Fake-Outs and launch dates are measured from closes; `charts/cases.json` holds the numbers.
- **Coin points:** these are figures reported at the time.
- **Computed figures:** where a market cap is price × circulating supply, the course says "estimate".
