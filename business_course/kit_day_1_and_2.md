[Show the proof on screen for a few seconds.]

But I want to tell you the part people don't post.

After that run, I nearly gave a big part of it back. [One honest sentence about what happened.]

So I wrote down every rule from my best trades and turned them into a system. Then I built a bot that follows those rules, on hundreds of markets, without ever getting bored, scared or greedy.

Most days, it does nothing. It skips almost everything. That's the point.

Every trade it takes goes on a public page. The wins and the losses.

**[WHO THIS IS FOR]**

I'm now working with a small number of people one-on-one, for ninety days.

It's for you if you've traded for at least six months, you've lost money or given back gains, and you're ready to follow rules instead of feelings.

It's not for you if you want a guarantee, or you're trading money you can't afford to lose. Nobody can promise you profits, and I won't.

**[WHAT TO DO NEXT]**

I can only take a few people at a time, because it's me on the calls.

If this sounds like you, click the button below and fill in the short application. It takes two minutes. If it looks like a fit, you'll pick a time and we'll talk about where you are and whether I can help.
