# Start here: your 14-day schedule

One rule: **do only today's box.** Don't read ahead, don't tidy, don't improve yesterday's work.

**Who does what**

| | |
|---|---|
| **I write** | Every script, message, email, page text, form question, checklist and template. You copy and paste. |
| **You do** | Record yourself, click "create" in the tools, post, and take the calls. Nobody else can do these. |
| **Hire out** | Video editing and thumbnails (one freelancer, from Day 8). |
| **Automated** | Reminders, DM replies, emails, onboarding (set up once in week 2). |

Each day is 1.5–3 hours. Things marked ✍ are already written for you in the kit files.

---

## Week 1: get the door open

The goal of week 1 is small: someone can apply, book a call and pay you.

### Day 1 · Open the door (2.5 h)
1. Create the mentorship on Whop: $5,000 in full, or 2 × $3,000. ✍ description in the Day 1 kit.
2. Make the application form (Typeform or Whop form). ✍ 5 questions in the kit.
3. Make a Calendly event: 45 minutes, next 4 days only, 3 slots a day. Put the form in front of it.
4. Start a Google Sheet. ✍ column names in the kit.
5. Send the warm message to 10 people. ✍ in the kit.

### Day 2 · Tell your story (2 h)
1. Record the page video on your phone, one take per section. ✍ script in the kit.
2. Post the 5-slide journey story sequence and save it as a highlight called "My story". ✍ slide text in the kit.
3. Put the application link in your Instagram bio.

### Day 3 · First long video (3 h)
1. Record YouTube video 02, the doctor story, using your script and `00_HOW_TO_RECORD.md`.
2. Send the warm message to 10 more people. Reply to yesterday's answers.

### Day 4 · Proof (1.5 h)
1. Make a "Proof" highlight: your real screenshots, the bot results page, testimonials you have permission to use.
2. Post one reel: 30 seconds of you saying the hook from video 02.
3. Take any calls that are booked. ✍ call checklist arrives before Day 4.

### Day 5 · After-booking video (2 h)
1. Record the after-booking video (10–15 min). ✍ script arrives before Day 5.
2. Add it to the Calendly confirmation page.
3. Post one reel.

### Day 6 · Batch day (3 h)
1. Film 4 reels in one sitting. ✍ hooks arrive before Day 6.
2. Film YouTube video 03 (why I built a bot).

### Day 7 · Review (1 h)
1. Fill in the sheet: people messaged, applications, calls booked, calls taken, sales.
2. Tell me the numbers. I'll tell you the one thing to fix.
3. Rest.

**End of week 1 target:** 20 people messaged, 3–5 calls booked, 1 YouTube video recorded.

## What to expect, honestly

- Your first sales will come from **people who already follow you**, through the warm messages and stories. YouTube takes weeks to start sending buyers.
- A realistic first month is **2–4 students** ($10K–$20K collected), not a flood. That is a good first month in his own numbers.
- Skip the course until you have students. It's the downsell, and it can wait until week 3.
- The trading bot is not touched by any of this.
