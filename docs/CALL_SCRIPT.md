# The PRNTR call script (45 minutes, on Zoom)

Built on HanckFX's closing structure (intro → discovery → pain → pre-handle objections → pitch → price → objections
→ pay on the call) with PRNTR's offers. Read it a few times, then say it your way: the words in *italics* are
examples, not lines to read.

**The goal of the call is not to sell.** It's to find out whether you can help them. If you can, you show them how
and let them decide. If you can't, you say so. That honesty is what makes people buy, and what stops chargebacks.

---

## 1. Open (2 minutes)

> *"Hey [name], good to meet you. Quick thing first: I record these so you can rewatch it later, that cool?"*
> (wait for yes)
>
> *"So here's how this works. I'm going to ask you a bunch of questions about your trading, where you are, where you
> want to be. If I think I can help, I'll show you exactly how. If I don't think I can, I'll tell you, and I'll point
> you to what I'd do instead. Sound fair?"*

Their "yes" here matters: they've agreed you'll make a recommendation at the end.

---

## 2. Discovery (15 minutes) — where they are

Ask, then **shut up and listen**. One question at a time. Write down their words.

1. *"What made you book this call, why now?"*
2. *"How long have you been trading, and what are you trading?"* (memecoins, leverage, both)
3. *"Walk me through a normal week of trading for you."*
4. *"What have you tried so far? Courses, signals, YouTube, Discords?"* → *"How did that go?"*
5. *"Roughly, are you up, down or about even over the last few months?"*
6. *"What's the last trade that really hurt? What happened?"*

Follow every answer with **"tell me more about that"** or **"what do you mean by that?"** at least once.

---

## 3. Pain and goal (10 minutes) — why it matters

1. *"You wrote that your biggest struggle is '[their words]'. How long has that been going on?"*
2. *"What has it cost you so far? Not just money, time, stress, how you feel after a red day?"*
3. *"If nothing changes in the next 6 months, where does that leave you?"*
4. *"Now flip it. Ninety days from now, if this all clicked, what does that look like? What's different?"*
5. *"Why is that important to you?"* (ask it twice; the second answer is the real one)
6. *"On a scale of 1 to 10, how committed are you to actually fixing this?"* → *"Why not a 10?"*

**Summarize it back:**
> *"So let me make sure I've got it. You've been trading [time], mostly [market]. The problem is [their struggle,
> their words], it's cost you [cost], and what you actually want is [their goal] because [their why]. Did I get that
> right?"*

---

## 4. Pre-handle the objections (2 minutes) — before you pitch

> *"Before I tell you how I'd help: if what I show you makes sense and it's a fit, is there anything that would stop
> you from starting this week? Money, time, needing to talk to anyone?"*

Whatever they say, deal with it **now**, not after the price:
- **Partner / spouse:** *"Would they want to hear this too? We can get them on, or I can send you the recording."*
- **Time:** *"It's about 30–60 minutes a day plus a call every two weeks. Can you make that work?"*
- **Money:** *"Got it. I'll show you the options, there are a few levels and payment plans."*

---

## 5. The pitch (5 minutes) — tied to what they told you

> *"OK. Based on everything you said, here's what I'd do with you.*
>
> *It's 90 days 1-on-1 with me. We get on a call every two weeks, six calls, and we go through your actual trades.
> You can message me in between. You get the full PRNTR course: the memecoin side (security, the rug check, judging
> wallets, the profit ladder) and the leverage side (position size, the checklist, risk rules, paying yourself).
> And you get every signal my bot sends for the 90 days, so you see the system work in real time while you learn it.*
>
> *The reason I think it fits you: you said [their struggle]. That's exactly [the part of the program that fixes
> it]. And you want [their goal]. By the end of the 90 days you'll have your own written plan for that."*

Then ask, before the price:
> *"How does that sound to you?"* (let them talk) *"What part of that matters most to you?"*

Screen-share if it helps: the bot's public track record (losing trades included) and one student result.

## 6. The price — start at their tier, step down only on a "no"

> *"The 1-on-1 is $2,997, or you can split it into two payments of $1,597 or three of $1,097. Which works better
> for you?"*

Then **stop talking**. Let the silence sit.

**The downsell ladder** — only when they genuinely can't do the level above, one step at a time:

1. **Group, $1,997** (2 × $1,097 / 3 × $747):
   > *"If the 1-on-1 isn't doable right now, there's the group mentorship. Same course, same signals, same 90 days,
   > but the calls are group calls with me instead of 1-on-1. That's $1,997, or split in two or three."*
2. **Course, $997** (2 × $547):
   > *"The other option is the course on its own, $997. No calls and no signals, but it's the full system at your own
   > pace, and you can move up later."*
3. **Signals, $199 / month:**
   > *"Or if now just isn't the time for a program, you can start with the signals at $199 a month, cancel any time,
   > and see how the system trades before you commit to more."*

**Odd amounts** (a deposit, a custom split): make it in seconds on **/pipeline → Make a payment link**. A deposit of
$250–500 "to hold your spot", with the rest within a few days, counts toward the program.

## 7. Objections — answer, don't argue

Always start with **"That makes sense"**, then a question.

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| *"I need to think about it."* | *"Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through? Let's do it now while I'm here."* (usually it's money or doubt; handle that) |
| *"It's too expensive."* | *"Compared to what? What has [their struggle] cost you so far?"* … *"Would a payment plan make it doable?"* → then the ladder |
| *"I need to talk to my wife / husband."* | *"Of course. What do you think they'll say? What would their concern be?"* (answer it, or offer to get them on a call) |
| *"Can I just get the signals?"* | *"You can, they're $199 a month. But signals without the system is how most people lose: they size wrong, skip stops, chase late entries. What I'm offering is the skill, not just the alerts."* |
| *"Is this a scam? / How do I know it works?"* | *"Fair question, there's a lot of that out there. Everything I do is public: the bot's track record with every losing trade, my students, the free playbook. And you've got 14 days: if the program isn't what I described today, you get a full refund."* |
| *"I don't have time."* | *"How much time do you spend trading now? This is 30–60 minutes a day, done right, instead of hours of guessing."* |
| *"I've been burned by courses before."* | *"What happened with that one?"* … *"That's exactly why this is 1-on-1 on your own trades, not videos you watch alone."* |
| *"Will I make my money back?"* | *"I can't promise that and nobody honest can. Trading is risky and some trades lose. What I promise is the coaching, the calls, the system and the signals. What you do with them is up to you."* |

## 8. The yes: confirm, then pay on the call

**The three confirmations** — on the recording, wait for each "yes":
1. *"Just so we're clear: I can't promise you'll make money. Nobody honest can. What I promise is the coaching, the
   calls, the course and the signals. You're OK with that?"*
2. *"If in the first 14 days, and before our second call, it isn't what I described, you get a full refund. After
   that there are no refunds. Fair?"*
3. (payment plan only) *"The plan is the full price split up, not a subscription you can stop. You're committing to
   all [two / three] payments. Good?"*

**Then send ONE link** (the plan they chose) in the Zoom chat and **stay on while they pay**:
> *"I've just sent the link. At checkout it'll ask you to type your full name, that's you signing the student
> agreement, it says exactly what we just went through. It'll show on your statement as WHOP-star-PRNTR."*

When it goes through:
> *"Welcome in. You'll get an email in a minute with how to get your Discord access and book our first call. Let's
> get to work."*

---

## 9. If it's a no

- **Not a fit** (beginner with no money, wants you to trade for them, under 18): *"Honestly, I don't think this is
  right for you right now, and I'd rather tell you than take your money. Start with the free playbook and the free
  Discord, and when things change, reach out."*
- **Fit, but not today:** *"No pressure. Can I send you the recording and check in on [day]?"* Book the follow-up
  before you hang up.

---

## 10. Straight after the call (1 minute)

On **/pipeline**, on their row:
- Stage: **showed** → **pitched** → **won** / **lost**, plus **downsold** if they took a lower tier.
- **Objection:** the main one (price, think about it, partner, trust, timing, fear, wants to do it alone).
- **Note:** anything you'll want next time.

That's what drives the follow-ups (lost → the signals offer next day, no-show → VN5 and re-book) and what tells you
which videos to make next.

## Never say

- "You'll make your money back", "guaranteed", "easy", "everyone makes money", income or win-rate numbers you can't
  show on the public track record.
- "Only today" or "last spot" unless it's true.
- Never push someone to pay on the call who's clearly not sure. A pressured buyer is the one who disputes.
