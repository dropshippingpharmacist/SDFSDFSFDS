## 4. Content system (the part only you can do)

- **YouTube, 6 videos a month** (every 5 days), each 15–30 min, tagged before filming:
  - 2 × top of funnel: broad problems ("why 90% of memecoin traders get rugged"), CTA: the free playbook (DM PRINTER)
  - 2 × middle: how you think, trade recaps, your story, CTA: free Discord
  - 2 × bottom: student case studies, "my system vs signals groups", objection videos from /pipeline, CTA: apply
- **Documentary series** (his #1 format): "Road to $1M in payouts with my AI bot", Day 1…N on IG daily, weekly YouTube episode. Proof first (bot's track record, payouts), losing days included.
- **IG**: 1–3 reels a day (studio clips), 3–5 story sequences a week (hook → retention → reward → "DM PRINTER"), every sequence saved to a highlight (Results, Students, The Bot, Start Here).
- **TikTok**: repurposed reels only (Repurpose.io), never extra work.
- Packaging: CTR 5–8%+, simple repeatable thumbnail, money/result in the title, hook in 5–15 s, change the visual every 20 s.
- Add `?src=yt-<video>` to every link so /pipeline shows which video makes money; the checkout also asks "which video made you join".

## 6. Decisions only you can make

1. **Guarantee** (he uses one on every landing page). Safest form: *"Follow the program (every call, the journal, the rules) and if you're not consistent after 90 days, I keep working with you free until you are."* Money-back versions need a lawyer.
2. **Group length**: 90 days or 6 months.
3. **Deposits**: a $250–500 deposit to hold a spot (counts toward the program); he says deposits close at a high rate.
4. **Booking window**: in Calendly set "max 4 days out", 45-minute calls, and put the link in `config.js`.
5. **Record the two VSLs** (scripts below) and add 3–5 student videos to `PRECALL_PROOF`.
6. **Sales team**: not yet. He's clear: the founder closes until ~$20–30k/mo, then a setter first, then a closer (10%), setter (5%).

## 7. VSL scripts

**Book-a-call VSL (5–8 min, sells only the click on "Apply")**
1. Hook (first 10 s): "If you've been trading 6+ months and you're still not consistent, this is for you. This is not a signals group."
2. Who I am: the $7K → $1M trade, then leaving medicine to make it consistent; the bot's public track record.
3. "This isn't X, this is Y": not another course or Discord; 90 days on *your* trades.
4. 5–7 pain points they'll recognize (rugged, liquidated, gave back a 10x, revenge trading, no system, alone).
5. Three student stories, pain → result (with "results not typical").
6. What happens next: apply (2 min), I read it, if it's a fit you pick a time this week. A few spots a month.
7. CTA: "Click Apply below."

**Post-booking VSL (3–5 min, sells only the show-up)**
1. "You're booked. Here's how to make this call worth it."
2. What we'll do on the call (your trades, your goal, what's stopping you) — no deliverables, no price.
3. One student who was exactly where they are.
4. "Bring your last 5 trades. Reply YES to the email/text so I know you're coming."
