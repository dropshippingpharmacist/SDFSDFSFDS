## 4. exactly how to make $100k+ selling trading courses (full blueprint)

**CONTENT**
- Only two platforms matter: YouTube (long-form educational) + Instagram (short-form authenticity-driven). "You need both to start." TikTok/X "not really relevant".

- Good content = educates, entertains, inspires, real value. Rule: 80-90% value, sell only 10-20%. Pitch the mentorship only in MOF/BOF YouTube videos (avoid looking like a guru).

**FUNNEL (Nurturing)**
- Nurture mechanisms: free Discord, email list, story sequences, MOF content, YouTube. Dig into pain points, tell your story, what changed, how you can help. Cold -> warm -> "extremely burning".
- Better nurture -> higher prices, higher AOV, higher LTV.

**SALES**
- "Good trading content and good nurturing, but bad sales equals $0 a month." Must know sales fundamentals to train a team.
- Under $10k/mo: take your own sales calls (with coaching). Reason: a sales rep on 10% of $5k/mo = $500/mo; "he won't work for you." Learn sales first, outsource later.
- Process: lead gen (DM / apply / book) -> qualification (questionnaire/GoHighLevel form: fit, "first-world country", invested, has money) -> 30-60 min sales call (bottlenecks, pre-handle objections, pitch, handle objections, close) -> payment -> onboard + contract.

**TEAM & OPS (Systems)**
- $0-10k/mo: manual, 40-50 hrs/week, founder does calls/DMs/fulfillment. Plateau zone $5k-30k/mo because founder is bottleneck (can't take more students, can't post more).
- Past $30k: automate onboarding, hire sales team, hire fulfillment team, batch content - "work on the business instead of inside it".

**DELIVERY & RETENTION (Fulfillment)**
- Prices referenced: $3k, $5k, $10k; some clients $20k.
- Fulfillment = foundation: enables testimonials, upsells, renewals, higher LTV, cross-sells, downsells; when students make more money "they run into another problem - you can sell towards that problem."
- Bad fulfillment -> refunds, bad reviews, no testimonials, reputation tanks.
- Hanck: posted "500 plus stories just about student results" on IG; flies clients out to record interviews/documentaries.

**METRICS (illustrative flywheel)**
- YouTube video 10,000 views -> 100 follows -> 30 days of stories/free Discord/freebie/emails -> 30 warm leads -> 12 book -> 9 show -> 4 close x $3k = $12k cash / $16k revenue.

**MISTAKES / DON'TS**
- Thinking you only need more content + better script + better program; expecting buyers after "a few videos" with no nurture bridge.
- Hiring a sales team below $10k/mo.
- Neglecting fulfillment after the sale.
- Most creators have content but miss nurture (no email/story sequences/MOF), sales team, systems, elite fulfillment.

## 6. How To Make A $100k Trading VSL (trading personal brands)

Hanck's own trading-mentorship VSL "does $50,000 a month". References: TDR, Alex G, Justin Werlein, Q Banks VSLs (they pay full-time copywriters).

**FUNNEL (VSL blueprint)**
- Purpose of VSL: "just to click this button" (Start Application / book a call). "Don't sell the program... sell the appointment." Selling happens later via post-booking VSL, pre-call VSL, reminders, closer intro, call.
- Psychology: mirror the pain of unprofitable traders -> build authority -> offer as solution -> remove risk -> create urgency. Speak to "frustrated traders who've tried everything" (not profitable / not funded / no payouts). Position as "anti-guru with real proof and vulnerability."
- 6-phase structure (as described; phases 4-5 not explicitly numbered - roughly social proof/case studies):
  1. Pattern interrupt/hook: shocking statement or guarantee. Hanck's open: "this right here is actually insane" + "I will guarantee that you become profitable and a six-figure funded trader... if you don't, I'll send you $1,000 back" + "this is not a 997 course, it's not a signals group". Use "This isn't X... this is Y" framing, e.g. "This isn't a group community with 500 traders. This is a one-on-one mentorship where I focus all my time on you and a small circle of traders"; "not a $997 guru course from Miami where I flex Rolexes or rented cars in Dubai." Set clear expectations what it is / isn't.
  2. Make them feel seen: list 5-10 specific pain points tailored to ICP (prop: losing challenges, no payouts, getting funded then blowing it; retail: depositing and blowing accounts; inconsistency; binge-watching YouTube with no results; endless backtesting; strategy hopping). Hit them emotionally.
  3. Your story/struggles + differentiate: position as real deal; "flame" fake gurus; "a program to help you become profitable from A to Z."
  (4/5) Social proof / case studies: e.g. 3 students; each: name + 2-3 specific pain points + transformation to specific result ("$0 to $10k a month", "$20k a month in 30 days"); show proof verbally AND visually (screenshots, Discord messages, interview clips).
  6. Offer + urgency to BOOK (not to buy): limited spots, be specific ("5 spots" 1:1, or 10/15; can later open 25-30 or group), why limited, what they get, guarantee/risk reversal. Urgency lines: "limited coaching capacity", "closes soon", "only available for five traders", "three already joined, only two spots left"; imply (indirectly) that waiting a week/two means missing it. CTA: "Start application" / "Get blueprint".

## 7. how to scale from $0 to $1M/month selling trading courses (5 phases)

Raw talking video. Phases: $0-10k, $10-30k, $30-100k, $100k-$1M, $1M+.

**Phase 2: $10-30k/mo**

- FUNNEL: simple - landing page, freebie, some email automations, maybe one VSL. No multi-step VSLs, no country-segmented sequences - still validating.

**Phase 3: $30-100k/mo ("whole new game")**

**Phase 4: $100k-$1M/mo**
- "Doing more of what already works" + tracking + bigger/better team + more systems; data-driven decisions.

**Phase 5: $1M+/mo**

## 8. how to make f*ck you money selling trading courses & mentorships

Proof: client made $450k+ in 2 months on Fanbases alone, + ~$100-120k on Whop ("web"), + ~$50-60k crypto (MetaMask, Trust Wallet). 3 pillars: Content, Back end, Results/testimonials.

**CONTENT**

**FUNNEL (5-step application funnel vs. simple book-a-call)**
- Don't push all traffic straight to a book-a-call page - no qualification.

- Qualified -> book-a-call page (VSL). Didn't book -> setters dial using application data and redirect to booking (some need a dial, more nurture, or more info).
- Booked -> post-booking VSL + landing page: pre-sell what's inside, what they get, make them "feel obligated to show up".
- All landing pages have VSLs.

**SALES**
- In-house closers ("been on QBanks, LXG, Omamba FX's offers") take calls, not the trader.

## Cross-video patterns in this batch

- **Kill low-ticket, go high-ticket.** In every case study the jump came from dropping $35-$700/mo Discords/low-ticket for $3k-$6k group / $5k-$20k 1:1 (Beppo $700 -> $25k; small-cap trader $3k -> $85.5k contracted; 20-yr-old $12k -> $40k cash). The thing in the way was the founder's limiting beliefs about price, not the size of the audience (~2k followers was enough for $51k collected).
- **YouTube long-form makes the money, short-form brings the eyeballs.** 90% of leads and the best leads come from YouTube BOF/MOF videos (20-50 min, backtests, weekly recaps). Target 1-2 quality videos/week (every 3-6 days); IG short-form 1-5/day; TikTok/X optional. "Views ≠ money": low-view BOF videos outsell viral TOF.
- **A day-by-day documentary/series format** ("Road to $1M in payouts") is the standout content play: easy ideas, binge-watching back to Day 1, and built-in proof.
- **Standard 3-tier ladder:** downsell (course/mid-ticket $500-$1k) -> main (group coaching, the scalable core) -> upsell (1:1, also used as a price anchor, e.g. $40k 1:1 makes $6.5k group "irresistible"). Fixed 3-6 month terms instead of lifetime, so students renew and feel urgency. Cap 1:1 at ~10 students, then raise its price and push group.
- **Payment structure:** push pay-in-full; make the payment-plan markups climb steeply (e.g. $5k PIF / 2x$3.25k / 3x$2.33k). Nobody pays under ~$1.3-2.3k per installment ("anything below 1.5k = annoying clients"). Track and actively collect outstanding plans: in his examples cash-to-revenue runs about 60-67%.
- **Funnel at scale:** application + VSL (qualify on capital and country) -> book-a-call -> post-booking VSL -> pre-call assets matched to the prospect's pain -> heavy reminders (from 2 days out down to 1 minute, plus a no-show ping) -> closer intro -> call. DQs get downsold. People who apply but don't book get dialed.
- **Setter/dialer is the fix for show rate and lead quality.** Booking-form DQ automations get gamed, so a human dials every lead. Benchmarks: 80-90% show, sub-20% DQ, 30-40% close. Without a post-booking VSL expect 10-20% close and ≤60% show.
- **Stage-appropriate building.** Under $10k/mo you only need content + a calendar link, and you take your own calls. Reason: a rep on 10% of $5k makes $500 and won't stay. From $10-30k/mo add a simple landing page, freebie and emails. From $30k/mo stop 1:1, go group with sub-coaches, and hire a closer, setter and sales manager. Past $100k/mo it's tracking and a bigger team (community manager for refunds/disputes/onboarding, CSMs).
- **Hiring:** closers should have 5+ years and millions closed on trading offers. Students are only good for community manager/CSM roles. Don't hire friends. Per-rep dashboards drive firing and bonuses, and closers leave if the founder stops posting content.
- **The data loop goes back into content:** end-of-call reports show objection patterns. "Not nurtured enough" means post more MOF/BOF; "too pricey" means adjust price. VSL conversion gains (20% -> 40%) can double revenue at scale.
- **Contracts and conditional guarantees:** guarantee like "$1,000 back" or a full refund, conditional on attendance, modules watched, 1:1 calls booked, minimum eval spend, minimum trades per week and journaling. Onboarding is automated: payment -> CRM "closed" -> contract (DocuSign) -> access link.
- **Recurring tools:** GoHighLevel (CRM/funnels; never send email from it because it lands in spam), ConvertKit/Kit for email, Whop (high fees), Wise/Fanbases (lower fees), crypto wallets, ManyChat (setter on IG), vidIQ + Claude (ideation from competitor transcripts), URL Genius (IG -> YouTube app), Zapier, Fathom, Looker Studio/Sheets.
- **The proof-first VSL** sells the appointment, not the program: hook/guarantee -> "this isn't X, this is Y" -> 5-10 specific pain points -> story + anti-guru positioning -> 3 case studies (pain -> result, shown visually) -> limited-spots urgency to book.
