## 1. I copied TJR's funnel and made $75k/mo selling trading courses (FULL COURSE)

- OFFER
  - Signal groups are "outdated and saturated" — people "buy into people", not signal groups; switch to a personal trading brand (coaching/mentorship).

  - Price creates commitment: getting a student to pay "$5 to $8 to $10,000" makes them "locked in" and get results; he's seen trading offers at $15k–$25k.
  - His own offer (~$50k/mo): landing header "Become a six-figure funded and profitable trader" + guarantee "in less than four months" + risk reversal "if you don't succeed, I'll personally send you $1,000 back".
  - Application money question framing: "this is not a course, not a signals group. It's a one-on-one coaching built to make a six-figure funded trader. How much are you ready to invest to achieve that?"
  - Downsell: DQ leads → mid-ticket **$500–$750, sometimes $1k**; then a further downsell if they can't afford that.
  - Financing is key: a $4k high-ticket 1:1/group program averages **$3,000 AOV** due to financing/payment options (names Fanbasis and Wise connections).
- FUNNEL
  - Content mapped to funnel stages: TOF (lifestyle, broad "how to start day trading" — awareness only, don't sell), MOF (educational how-tos, playlists — nurture), BOF (raw live trading/trade recap videos — convert). TJR alternates TOF/BOF/MOF in upload pattern.
  - YouTube homepage playlists (e.g. "Boot Camp", "Trading Transformation") to binge-nurture.
  - Cold → warm → hot; "you don't sell people in the cold."
  - Free value formatted so viewer subconsciously feels there's a gap ("barrier") only the program fills — without explicit CTA.
  - Landing page linked from YouTube description, Discord, Instagram (+ occasional CTAs). Opt-in (first name, email, phone) gates the VSL → enables email + SMS automations.
  - Fix: verify email (send confirm link by email) or you get flooded with fake emails.
  - Landing page structure: header (brand/"the blueprint") → "Step 1: watch this quick video explaining what [program] is and who it's for" → VSL → "Start application" button, all on one page.
  - VSL's ONLY job is to get the click to "Start application" — touch pain points, make them realise they need help. Not to explain/sell the offer.
  - Flow: landing page → application → DQ (→ mid-ticket downsell → further downsell) or Qualify → book-a-call → post-booking VSL → pre-call VSL → appointment reminders (SMS/email/WhatsApp) + content pieces → call by sales rep → KPI sheet.

  - Note under it: "invite might land in spam… I need you to reply YES or READY to the email to 100% confirm your appointment. Failure to respond may lead to cancellation" (not actually enforced) → moves emails out of spam + raises show rate.
  - Reminders contain copy + case studies/testimonials of people "in their exact position"; they use AI to scan application (sometimes two application forms) to match testimonials to lead.

  - Short form = TOF/awareness; YouTube long form = nurture/convert, "where the real money is at".

  - Big numbers in titles drive clicks ("$75,000" vs "$2,000").

  - "Content and getting eyeballs is the biggest lever you can ever pull"; backend doesn't work without front-end traffic.

## 2. How To Make VIRAL Trading Content (step-by-step guide)

- CONTENT

  - Benchmarks: CTR **8–12% at launch**, **5–7%** later as it goes broad. AVD minimum **4–5 min for a 10–15 min video**; 2–3 min = fix retention. 10–15 min video with **5–6 min AVD + 8–12% CTR** → "best believe that video is going to get over 100,000 views".
  - His launch metrics: **13% CTR, ~6 min AVD** in first 24h; after ~100k views CTR dropped to **4.5%**, AVD ~5 min (on 18-min video).
  - Thumbnail: must stop the scroll, promise value, create curiosity. "Flashy but authentic": bold, high contrast, simple — his winner = **two candlesticks + arrows (e.g. "liquidity grab") + flashy lifestyle photo** (villa/Croatia/Greece). Reused the same concept on his top 4 videos (130k, ~40k, ~20k, ~20k). "Once you find something winning, milk that." Copy what works, add your touch.
  - Rule: "would I click on this?" — if you hesitate, redo.
  - Titles: promise clear outcome + money + simple/relatable: "This one candle strategy changed my life ($100,000 in profits)", "How I made $10,000 trading prop firms". Curiosity + value: "The secret strategy top traders don't want you to know". Match title to content (no clickbait — kills AVD). Keep simple.
  - Hook: first **5–10 sec** on YouTube, **1–2 sec** on short form. Framework: **Hook → Retention → Reward**.
  - Use **visual hook** (results, payout screenshots, student results, annotated charts) + **verbal hook** simultaneously.
  - Stack many pain points (he hit ~11–12 in the intro): "It took me 3 years to become profitable… constantly blowing accounts… feeling stuck… binge watching YouTube 10/12/14 hours a day… losing money… Zoom meetings in the middle of the night… tried all strategies, all indicators, all pairs, all sessions, all timeframes" — with BTS photos from 2021. More pain points = more of the audience feels addressed.
  - Promise transformation up front ("I'll break down the exact strategy… from A to Z") so they know you'll deliver if they stay.
  - Don't give away the full strategy in the first 30 sec–2 min.
  - Retention: never let the screen sit still **>20 sec**; switch view/topic every **1–3 min** (intro → theory → phase 1 → phase 2 → chart examples → candlestick view 30m/15m bullish/bearish → trade recaps x3 → live trading examples → CTA). Constantly draw/annotate/zoom. "Show and tell". Re-state pain + solution mid-video ("I know you're frustrated blowing accounts, but…").
  - Give ~80–90% free, keep 10–20% behind paywall (mentorship).
  - Reward/CTA at end: free **16-page PDF playbook** via opt-in (email, maybe phone) → email automations → book-a-call funnel.
  - End screens pointing TOF video viewers to MOF and BOF videos.
  - Pre-post checklist: thumbnail eye-catching/clear; title compelling; hook strong visually + verbally; retention optimized; deliver promise; reward/CTA at end.
  - Track data and iterate "just like trading".
- METRICS
  - 18-min video: 130k views; ~six figures revenue; CTR 13%→4.5%; AVD ~6→~5 min.
- MISTAKES / DON'TS
  - Clickbait mismatched titles; static screens; front-loading all value; hooking with only one pain point.

  - Example: 100 booked calls × 60% show × 25% close ≈ $52k; raise to 80% show & 35% close → ~2x revenue from same leads (he says "80% left" — unclear).

  - $100k/mo: divide by 10 → 10k YouTube views/month (he says 100k views / 6 videos ≈ 18k per video for $1M, and "1.8k views per video" for $100k). Post 6 videos/month (every 5 days).
  - Their actual: visit-to-book **~10.8%**; AOV ~$5.1k; show >80%; close >50%.
  - Below $10k/mo the small metrics don't matter — focus fundamentals; "to get to 10k per month you need to sell two people a 5k trading mentorship".
  - At $1.6M/mo, +5–10% close rate ≈ almost $2M.
  - Track only **YouTube** views for acquisition (not IG/TikTok/Twitter) — "YouTube… is what's going to drive the needle".
  - JJ starting traffic: ~20k YT views/mo, 5% view-to-visit, 5% visit-to-book.

  - At ~100k/mo: **1 setter, 1 closer + sales manager** (manager hires, vets, trains, onboards, does call reviews, 1:1 coaching reviews, group coaching calls).

## 10. Why I shut down my $102k/m business (DFY agency)
