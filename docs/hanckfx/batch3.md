## 3. How To Build A $3K - $5k Trading Mentorship/Program (step-by-step)

**OFFER — positioning & pricing logic**
- $5k is "not expensive to the right buyer": target traders struggling 6-12+ months (pass funded accounts then blow them, never get payouts). Beginners (trading ~1 month) won't buy high ticket — "they haven't had enough pain points hit yet."
- Sell "a shortcut to competence" / "a road map on what they need to do, how ... when" — not "trading education" ("who cares about a trading course nowadays"). "Your price is justified by the structure, the access, and the risk reduction."
- Low ticket (Discord subs, $95-99/mo): low-commitment buyers (he names India/Africa), high churn, high refunds/disputes, big support team, harder to get transformations, churn after one losing trade. Hanck: low-ticket Discord made $1,500/mo -> switched to high ticket -> $51k two months later; ~3 disputes lifetime on low ticket vs zero on high ticket.
- High ticket benefits: higher-intent buyers, faster results (financially invested, "no plan B") -> more proof -> more testimonials/case studies -> more sales; fewer clients, higher margins, less customer service.
- Two high-ticket models:
  - 1:1 mentorship: easiest to sell/market (direct access), slightly faster results, stronger relationship, ~90%+ profit margin; limited above ~$50k/mo (capacity ~20-40 people with bi-weekly calls). Typically 3-4 months in trading niche.
  - Evergreen group coaching: scalable to $250k-$1M+/mo; longer retention (3-6+ months, "ideally 6 months plus"); community momentum; coaches under you; ~70% profit margin; needs more team ops, lots of traffic and proof.
  - Can run group as main offer with 1:1 as upsell.
- Correct order: (1) run 1:1 first — learn fulfillment/coaching (accountability, calling out mistakes, rewarding), build testimonials; (2) systemize — put knowledge into docs, SOPs, modules, templates; (3) at $50k+/mo add group coaching + coaches to increase capacity.
- Payment plans: ~40% of buyers take a plan (rest PIF). Example: $5k PIF vs $2k x3 = $6k.
- Phased access to protect LTV on payment plans: Phase 1 (first 30 days) = optimize strategy + psychology; Phase 2 (after 30 days) = get funded; Phase 3 (after 60 days) = scaling + copy trading across multiple accounts. PIF buyers get everything instantly; plan buyers unlock phase by phase so they don't cancel after paying $2k. Tell prospects about the phases upfront. Whop supports drip/module access after X days.
- Drip even for PIF (e.g., ~1 hour of modules per day) to prevent overwhelm.
- Mid-ticket example: yearly subscription with a module "if you're still not profitable, read this" = upsell mechanism into 1:1 coaching.

**DELIVERY & RETENTION**
- Navigation document per phase: welcome/introduction, video links, doc links, step-by-step path.
- Every time a question is asked multiple times, make a module/doc/SOP and redirect (reduces support load). Example: added a "basics" category (what is a strategy, how to read candlesticks) even though ICP was intermediate.
- Module examples: welcome, basics, foundations, volume & session timing, market structure.
- Program components: resources, modules, week-by-week roadmaps, onboarding doc (ask a few questions, lay out a roadmap), links hub, templates, checklists, playbook + journal templates, live trading (record and add to Whop modules), Discord community (random chats, networking).

**CONTENT**

**METRICS**
- Traders with 4-6k YouTube subs making $50-70k/mo; Hanck claims >$100k/mo with ~1,300 subs. Client scaled $15-20k/mo -> $300k+/mo (also via back-end systems, automations, sales team, not just switching to high ticket).
- Margins: 1:1 90%+, group ~70%.

**MISTAKES / DON'TS**
- Targeting beginners with high ticket; selling "information"; giving plan buyers full access on day one; running low ticket.

## 4. ranking trading content formats in 2026 (that actually sells)

Criteria: virality, trust building, conversion, production time. Based on tracked lead/revenue data across clients (">$10M" claimed).

**CONTENT — Short form (IG/TikTok) tier list**
- S tier — Series/challenge documenting a journey (e.g., "$100 flip into $10,000", day 1, day 2...; client "path to $1M in payouts — day 7, $88k", ~10k likes). Binge-worthy: a viewer landing on day 7 goes to profile and watches day 1 onward. Insane conversion + trust, easy virality. Cons: needs dedication/discipline and you must actually be a good trader. "Our trading clients have made multiple six figures per month using this format."
- A tier — Green-screen talking (face over screenshot of trade, ~5 min to make): fewer views (~200-400 likes) but converts well; feels personal, unscripted; messaging reaches intermediate traders.
- B tier — Split-screen educational (webcam top/bottom + hook e.g. "This one candle is responsible for over a million dollars in profit" + ManyChat CTA at end): good for views, attracts beginners, weak trust/conversion, oversaturated ("better 2 years ago"); 15-30 min to make, ~1 h if self-edited.
- B tier — TikTok talking head (hook + captions, ~1-2 min to make): can hit ~30-100k views; broad/top-of-funnel, weak conversion, good trust, best production time.
- B tier — "Brain rot" larping (Rolex, Lambo, Dubai, Miami): easiest virality, attracts beginners, "absolutely zero conversion," no trust.

**CONTENT — Long form (YouTube) tier list**
- S tier — Series/challenge long form (e.g., "trading with $10 to prove it's not luck", "$100 to $1M in payouts in 2 years"); client Tom's got ~700k views. Highest virality, conversion and trust.
- A tier — Educational/strategy breakdown (e.g., "If you only watch one trading video, make this worth it", "the exact strategy that got me $1.2M in payouts"): numbers in the title help virality; top-of-funnel, low conversion; include live trading examples/trade recaps.
- A tier — Live trading / trade recaps: raw, unscripted, easiest to produce (example with black bars still got ~7k views); middle-of-funnel; FOMO + credibility; show losses too (proof matters because of fake gurus). Don't spam — hard to go viral, starves new leads.
- A tier — Psychology/mindset/storytelling (struggles, how you became profitable): converts "through the roof," trust "all-time high," easy to make; can only post ~once a week / few times a month (you run out of stories).
- B tier — Day-in-the-life/vlog (Dubai, R8): high views, low conversion, builds some behind-the-scenes trust, hard to produce regularly. Not necessary: client Tom Trades has never posted anything flashy and does "over quarter million a month."

**Strategy**
- No format wins all four criteria; mix formats as an ecosystem: TOFU (broad, views, beginners) -> MOFU/BOFU (trade recaps, case studies, psychology) to convert. "You don't want to be famous just for the sake of being famous."

**MISTAKES / DON'TS**
- Chasing views with larp/flex content; relying only on split-screen ManyChat reels; spamming one format.

## Cross-video patterns in this batch

- Revenue staircase with offer change at each step: 1:1 mentorship to ~$10-30k/mo (some say up to $50k), then systemize delivery into SOPs/modules, then group mentorship + coaches beyond $30-50k; 1:1 with founder survives as the upsell, course-only as the downsell. Low-ticket Discord subscriptions are rejected at every stage.
- Pricing benchmarks: 1:1 ~$4k for 3-4 months (AOV ~$2.5k); group ~$5-6k for 4-6 months (AOV ~$3k); founder 1:1 upsell ~$9.8k; course downsell ~$2k. Payment plans always add up to more than PIF (e.g., $6k PIF vs $3.5k x2 vs $2.8k x3), plus a "PIF clearance" discount within 30 days and phased/drip access (unlock phase 2 at day 30, phase 3 at day 60) so plan buyers keep paying.
- Cash-to-revenue target is 60-70%. Anything at 90%+ means the price is too low. Raising prices is "the easiest way to make more money."
- Sales KPI benchmarks repeat across videos: show rate 75-80%+ (90% with WhatsApp/IG reminders), close rate 30-35%+ when the founder closes and ~25% with a team at scale. Always work backward from revenue to clients, shows and meetings: $10k needs ~15 meetings/month, $30k ~43, $100k ~177.
- Founder closes until ~$20-25k/mo. A setter comes first, for show-ups, no-shows, disqualified-lead downsells, qualified-but-unbooked leads and reminders. A team of closers comes at ~$100k/mo.
- The pre-call nurture stack is consistent: post-booking VSL, 4-6 reminders that link case-study YouTube videos, pain-point pre-call videos triggered by application answers, WhatsApp/IG DMs (needs an IG field on the form), and fake-personal AI Loom and voice notes sent by the setter.
- Content: YouTube (especially case studies, series/challenges, trade recaps, psychology/story) converts far better than short form. Views do not equal money (example: $9.30/view vs $0.90/view). Series/challenge "documenting the journey" is the single best format on both short and long form. Larp/flex and split-screen ManyChat reels mainly attract beginners.
- Track everything in three pillars: content (revenue per video via attribution software, YouTube KPIs, device split), back end (VSL play rate, CTA click, booking rate, emails) and sales (show, close, PIF rate, cash-to-rev, AOV, LTV, per-rep stats) in a Looker Studio dashboard. Fix the specific leak instead of rebuilding.

Remove the founder from fulfillment (written program, sub-coaches from successful students, bi-weekly instead of weekly calls, Discord support tickets instead of DMs).
