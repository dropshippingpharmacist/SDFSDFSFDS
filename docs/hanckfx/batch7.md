## 2. 20 lessons from making $20M selling trading courses

Context: $20M combined over ~16 months; one client $6.7–6.8M in ~6 months, now **$1.5M/mo** (was at $8k/mo on low ticket 6–7 months ago). His own business: ~$530k/mo at **81–82% margins**.

**TEAM & OPS**
- L1 "Fire yourself as the CEO": automate or delegate everything except your content. Content max **1–2 h/day** (editor, designer); fulfillment max **1–2 h/day** (ideally ≤1h); backend/sales/tracking **0 h** with team + automation.
- L9 Ask "can this scale?" — taking every call yourself, 1:1 time-for-money, doing everything with no team, manual onboarding, manual tracking/fixing backend = not scalable. Test: "can I get on 100 people tomorrow or would it break?"
- Exception: **below $10k/mo take your own sales calls and sell your time**; above $10k/mo get a team.
- L12 You are the bottleneck at $5k/$10k/$20k/mo; five areas to streamline/automate/delegate: content, backend, sales, fulfillment, tracking.
- L14 Lean team of killers. Content: **1 editor + 1 thumbnail designer** (freelancers). Sales: **each closer + setter pair can do ~$100k/mo** (±$20k) → $300k/mo = 3 setters + 3 closers. 1 editor, 1 designer, 1 closer, 1 setter can get you to $100k/mo at ~85% margins.
- Fulfillment team / **CSM not needed until ~$250k/mo**; they only just onboarded a CSM at $1.5M/mo because group fulfillment had so much leverage; CSM job = upsell rate, renewals, cross-sells.
- **Don't hire friends or students as closers**; hire for skill, externally or from network.
- L17 Don't use growth operators (rev-share agencies) below **$250k/mo**; good ones won't take you (at $100k/mo a 20% share = $20k, not worth it for them); you won't learn your own business. His model: consulting for <$250k/mo, agency (DFY) only for >$500k/mo currently.
- L18 Track your business; decide on data not feelings (e.g., think close rate good but it's 30% — doubling it doubles revenue).

**METRICS**
- L2 $100k/mo at 80% margin ≈ $1M/yr profit → 10 years to $10M liquid; $10M at 5% = $500k/yr. "Aim bigger."
- L3 Six bottlenecks in high ticket: **content views → landing page visitors → book-a-call rate → show rate → close rate → AOV**. Reverse-engineer from revenue goal.
- L15 example: $6M via low ticket ($100 AOV, $300 LTV, i.e. ~33% churn) = 20,000 students; via high ticket (AOV $6k, actual "over $5,000"; LTV "over $9k") = <1,000 students. Client had ~35k IG + ~30k YouTube (65k total).
- Dutch client: $3k/mo ($100/mo Discord) → **$70k+/mo with 1,000 YouTube subs and 3.5k IG**. Another: $7k/mo → **$140k/mo with ~5k YouTube**, barely uses IG.
- One YouTube video made **$2M+** for a client.
- One client scaled to **$1.2M/mo all organic**.
- Podcasts/collabs made clients **$3–3.5M**.
- Buying journey: **most students buy within 1–4 weeks** after binge-watching (YouTube).

**CONTENT**
- L8 Ride momentum: when a video pops, milk it, repurpose, iterate on winners until it stops working (same for ads).
- L13 Best ideas = problems your younger self had (strategy, psychology, "eval jail", payouts) + current students' struggles (ask them, send a Typeform, 1:1/group calls); recurring patterns → content and group coaching topics.
- L16 Volume: at least **1–2 high-quality YouTube videos/week** (hours each), **minimum 7–14 reels/day on Instagram**, **minimum ~50 trial reels/week**. Most could 2–3x volume.
- L19 Collabs/podcasts with other traders (names: "Times of Tomorrow", "What's of Wisdom"(?), "Sharp and X" — unclear spellings).
- L20 **~90% value / 10% selling**; don't make every post a pitch.
- L6 Audience cares about details 10x less than you; stuttering doesn't matter.

**OFFER**
- L4 No USP = won't sell. USP can be strategy, risk approach, payouts, proof. Plus irresistible offer, ideally conditional guarantee with contracts.
- L15 Sell high ticket to intermediate/advanced traders, not low ticket to beginners (beginners: low pain, low capital, high refund rates, no commitment). High ticket → better payment-plan completion, higher LTV/AOV.
- L10 Spend weeks–quarters iterating fulfillment until it's best in niche → results → testimonials/interviews/case studies → content flywheel. (Spent 6+ months building their consulting program.)

**FUNNEL**
- L11 **No ads below $100k/mo** (even multi-six figures not needed); "organic is the fire, ads are the fuel"; consider ads at $100k–$500k/mo.

**MISTAKES / DON'TS**
- Perfectionism / overthinking (L5): "think less, do more"; business = find bottleneck, fix, hit next.
- Treating a low-ticket Discord as a business (L7).
- Low posting volume (1 YT video + 2 reels/week).
- Hiring friends/students; growth operators too early; ads too early; going off feelings.
