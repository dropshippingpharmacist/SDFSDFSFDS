- **9 types:**
  1. **Value-driven** (example 8 slides): S1 hook ("100K day — we made our trading client $100,000 in one day… watch until the end… this is genuinely getting out of hand") over a lifestyle/background image; S2 behind-the-scenes payment notifications; S3 FOMO (avg $71k/day last 4 days; client was at 8K/mo "inconsistent, annoyed" before); S4–6 value (how they helped: content, sales team, tracking); S7 authority ("speedran $1M PnL in ~45 days from zero"); S8 CTA tailored to what the sequence covered (content, offer, backend, mentorship, tracking) + pain-point line: "if you're not at a million dollar per month yet or even $100,000… you have not optimized every single aspect" → "take action instead of watching our clients scale past you because I know that sh*t irritates you." Soft CTA.
  2. **Story launch** (for small, mid or big audiences): 7-day pre-launch (can be 10 or 14 days); post every other day (days 1, 3, 5, 7). Days 1 & 3 = soft CTAs (to DMs / YouTube / interviews), build hype + FOMO with student results/journeys (e.g. "flew a group of students out to a villa", "18K in payouts, 40K in payouts", "My student Ron made $65,000 in his first three months… stuck, not knowing how to properly risk on prop firms"); end of day 1: "in a week I'll be launching something revolutionizing… backed by $1.6M in verified payouts" (tailor USP to the sequence). Days 5 & 7 = hard CTA = book a call. Soft vs hard CTA: soft → DMs/YouTube/interview; hard → book a call directly. Do a launch every 3–4 months (soft or hard), ideally student-result based.
  3. **Journey / storytelling** (took "a few hours" to make): sections — intro+proof ("we officially just hit $100,000"), background (his own trading brand: 15K subs, a few videos 100K+ views, $10k first month, later $50k/mo selling mentorships, then pivot to B2B), month 1, months 2–3, months 4–5 → CTA tailored to the story's theme (team/systems: "Stop trying to do everything yourself… let other people do it that are better than you").   8. **Case study** (3–5 slides; examples are 4 slides): hook e.g. "stuck at 20k/mo to now 100k/mo… in only 30 days" (curiosity), journey, retention, soft CTA to an interview or YouTube breakdown. Main use: drive IG short-form viewers to YouTube ("that's what's going to convert them").

## 3. High Ticket Trading Mentorships vs Low Ticket Trading Communities (full breakdown)

**OFFER**

- Structure high ticket with **phases**, payment plans, upsells, cross-sells, completion rates; CSM for accountability.
- Action: if you have low ticket → raise prices drastically, add coaching, or switch to high ticket fully. If starting → start high ticket, "build a premium brand from the get-go." Signals aren't sellable high ticket — package resources, skills and time into a mentorship.

**FUNNEL**
- Low-ticket funnel = straight VSL / checkout / Stripe / Whop / WhatsApp link. High-ticket = **book-a-call funnel + sales team + automations + appointment reminders**.
- System: content → leads → nurture (story sequences, email automations, IG DMs, bottom-of-funnel YouTube videos) → sales calls → closes.
- Free trial to Discord is worse: extra friction step (viewer → free trial → click → convert), lowest-quality leads, ~$600 vs $800.

**METRICS** (from clients' before/after averages, "rounded down")
- Low ticket: 100 viewers → 50 click → 8 buy (8%) → $800 at $100/mo; avg member stays 2–3 months (~2.5) → **LTV ~$200–250**; churn **30–40%/month**. To hit $10k/mo you need 100 sales/month (~25/week).
- High ticket: 100 viewers → **40% book-to-call** → 40 calls → **70–80% show** (use 70%) → 28 shows → **20–35% close** (industry avg ~30%) → 6–8 closes × **$2.5k AOV** (on a 4–5k ticket) = $15–20k → **18–25× more** revenue from same traffic. $10k/mo = 4 clients = 1 sale/week. High-ticket LTV **3k+**.

**DELIVERY & RETENTION**

**MISTAKES / DON'TS**
