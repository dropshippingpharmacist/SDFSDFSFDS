The PRNTR Mindset

Most people trade like they are playing a slot machine. PRNTR traders trade like a doctor follows a checklist. We wait for one very specific picture on the chart, we decide how much we can lose before we start, and we skip everything else. Skipping is the skill.

Reading the Chart Like a Story

A chart is just a story of what price did. Each candle is one chapter: where price started, how high it went, how low it went and where it ended. Put the chapters together and you see whether the story is going up, going down or going sideways.

The Box

After price runs up or down for a while, it gets tired and rests. While it rests, it bounces between a ceiling and a floor. We draw a box around that. The box is where everybody puts their stop-losses, and that is exactly what makes it useful.

Module 2

Why the box matters

Every box has two piles of stop-losses sitting just outside it.
  • Buyers' stops: just below the floor.
  • Sellers' stops: just above the ceiling.
  • Big players know exactly where they are.

Module 3

The Fake-Out and the Return

Big players need lots of orders to fill their big trades. The biggest piles of orders sit just outside the box. So price pokes outside the box, grabs those orders, and comes straight back in. That poke is the fake-out. When price comes back inside, it often runs all the way to the other side of the box.
