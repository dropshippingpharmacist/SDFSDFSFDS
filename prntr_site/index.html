The 90-day mentorship

One coach, one student, one plan. You finish trading by rules you understand, not by feel.

Weeks 1–4 · ProtectSet your account up properly. Risk per trade, position size, staying clear of liquidation. Take the signals at small size and journal every trade. Goal: 10 trades taken exactly by the rules.
Weeks 5–8 · BuildWhy a trade is taken and why most are skipped. Managing a trade once you're in. Moving profits into a real plan. Reviews of your trades on our calls. Goal: a full month without breaking a rule.
Weeks 9–12 · IndependenceYour own written trading plan. Scaling size slowly. You can explain every trade you take without me. Goal: trading on your own, with confidence.

Questions

How much is the mentorship?It depends on what you need, so we go through it on the call, with pay-in-full and payment-plan options. If the mentorship isn't right for you yet, there's a self-paced course.
How much time does it take?About 30–60 minutes a day: taking the signals, journaling, and the lessons. Plus a call with me every two weeks.
Do I need a big account?No. You learn to size every trade as a small percentage of your account, so the method works the same at $500 or $50,000.
When do the signals arrive?The moment a setup confirms, and only while price is still at the entry. If price has already moved away, the signal isn't sent.
What markets?Crypto, forex, indices, commodities, metals and stocks.
What happens after the 90 days?You keep the course for good. Students can keep the signals with a monthly plan, or continue working with me.
Can I lose money?Yes. Every trade has a stop loss and some trades lose. Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
