The 90-day mentorship

Most leveraged traders don't lose because they can't read a chart. They lose on entries they chased, stops they moved and positions that were too big. The mentorship fixes those three things, with the same rules my signal bot trades.

1 · The setupThe PRNTR Press, step by step: a clean range (the Box), the fake breakout that clears the stops (the Final Fake-Out), the higher low (the Launch Pad), and the line that has to close twice (the Trigger Line and the Double Close). You learn to spot it, grade it, and skip the ones that don't pass.
2 · The order, the stop and the sizeA limit order on the retest instead of chasing the candle, a stop where the idea is wrong, a target several times bigger, and a clock that cancels the order if price never comes back. Then the maths: a fixed risk per trade, the right position size at any leverage, and staying far from liquidation.
3 · Managing it and keeping itWhat 3 years of tested trades say about moving your stop to breakeven, taking profit early and holding to target. Your journal, your weekly review, when not to trade, and how you pay yourself from a winning month instead of giving it back.

How every trade works

The loss is decided before you enter. Most of the work is waiting, and skipping the trades that don't pass.

Why trading, and not betting or a store

I've seen people try all of it: sports betting, dropshipping, ecom stores. Here's how trading compares, honestly.

Trading with rulesSports bettingDropshipping / ecom
Who you answer toYou and your rulesThe sportsbook sets the oddsCustomers, suppliers, ad platforms, payment processors
What you controlEntry, stop loss, size and exit, before the trade startsOnly the pick. The game decides the restProduct, ads, shipping, refunds, support tickets
The built-in costFees and funding on each tradeThe book's margin is in every line you betAd spend, product cost, returns and chargebacks
Loss on one bad callSet by your stop loss, decided in advanceThe whole stakeAd budget and stock already spent
Who else is involvedNobody. No inventory, no employees, no customersThe book, which profits when you loseCustomers and suppliers, every day
HoursThe bot watches. You act when a setup confirmsGame schedulesWhenever something breaks

What I like about it

  • It's just you. Every result, good or bad, is yours to own, and nobody else can be blamed
  • Every trade has its risk decided before you enter, so a loss is a cost you accepted, not a surprise
  • No customers to answer, no stock to sell, no ad account to get banned
  • There's a quiet in it: you follow the plan, then you step away

To be straight with you: trading isn't easier or safer than these. Most people who trade lose money, and leverage makes losses bigger as well as wins. What it gives you is control and accountability, if you follow the rules.

Questions

How much is the mentorship?It depends on what you need, so we go through it on the call, with pay-in-full and payment-plan options.
How much time does it take?About 30–60 minutes a day: taking the signals, journaling, and the lessons. Plus a call with me every two weeks.
Do I need a big account?No. You learn to size every trade as a small percentage of your account, so the method works the same at $500 or $50,000.
When do the signals arrive?The moment a setup confirms, and only while price is still at the entry. If price has already moved away, the signal isn't sent.
What markets?Leverage on crypto first, plus forex, indices, commodities, metals and stocks: the same setup works on all of them.
Do I have to trade memecoins?No. The mentorship is leverage trading. My memecoin process is an optional bonus for students who want it, kept separate from your trading account.
What leverage will I use?Leverage is a setting, not a strategy. You size every trade from a fixed risk and your stop, so the leverage only changes how much margin you put up, and you stay far from liquidation.
What happens after the 90 days?You keep the course for good, and you can keep working with me.
Can I lose money?Yes. Every trade has a stop loss and some trades lose. Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
