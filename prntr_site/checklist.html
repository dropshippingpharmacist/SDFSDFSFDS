The 7-point checklist my bot runs before it risks a dollar

Most traders lose because they enter on a feeling. My bot can't. Every trade has to pass these seven checks. If one fails, it doesn't trade. It waits for the next one.

1

Patience first

The bot spends most of its time doing nothing. It waits until the market shows its hand instead of forcing a trade because it's bored.

Why: the money is made in a handful of great trades, not in being busy.

2

A clear story on the chart

It only looks at markets where price has a clear structure, with levels the market has respected more than once. A messy chart is a skip.

Why: if you can't explain what price is doing in one sentence, you can't plan a trade around it.

3

Know who's trapped

Every move needs someone on the wrong side. The bot looks for where the crowd is stuck and trades with the side that's in control.

Why: most retail traders are the liquidity big players need. The goal is to stop being it.

4

A level with history

It trades from areas where serious buying or selling has shown up before, never from a random line someone drew.

Why: big money leaves footprints, and it tends to come back to defend them.

5

Confirmation, not prediction

The bot doesn't guess where price will turn. It waits for the market to prove the idea first, and only then gets in.

Why: being early feels smart and usually costs money. Being confirmed is boring and pays.

6

The math has to work

Before any trade, it knows exactly where it's wrong and where it takes profit. If the reward isn't several times the risk, it passes.

Why: with the right math you can lose more trades than you win and still grow the account.

7

Discipline, every single time

Same risk on every trade. No chasing a move that already left. No trading against the bigger market. No exceptions because it "feels right".

Why: a bot never gets emotional. That's the real edge, and it's the one most people never build.

What the bot always skips

  • Charts with no clear structure
  • Trades with no proof yet, only a hunch
  • Anything where the reward isn't worth the risk
  • A move that already happened
  • Fighting the direction of the bigger market
