The method · the PRNTR Press

6 steps. Every one is a rule.

  1. The Box. Price goes sideways for at least a day.
  2. The Final Fake-Out. It breaks out, takes the stops, and closes back inside.
  3. The Launch Pad. The next push holds short of the fake-out.
  4. The Trigger Line. Price has to close past it twice.
  5. The order. A limit on the retest. Stop where the idea is wrong. Target several times the risk.
  6. Leave it alone. The loss is decided before the trade is on.
