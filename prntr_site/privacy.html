Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated 15 November 2026.

1. What we collect

  • When you ask for the free playbook or join the free Discord: your first name and email (and your Discord account through Whop).
  • When you apply: your name, email, phone number, Instagram handle (optional), and your answers about your trading, goals and budget.
  • When you book a call: what you enter in Calendly. Calls may be recorded.
  • When you buy: Whop collects your payment details; we receive your name, email, what you bought and payment status, never your full card number.
  • When you message us: your Instagram, Discord or email messages.
  • Automatically: basic technical data our servers log (IP address, browser) to keep the site secure and stop spam.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver what you asked for or bought: the playbook, your call, the course, signals and calls.
  • To decide whether a program is a good fit and to follow up about your application or call.
  • To send you emails (lessons, reminders and offers; unsubscribe any time) and, only if you ticked the box, texts (reply STOP any time).
  • To handle payments, refunds and disputes, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • We keep one record per email address so you don't get the same email twice.

3. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information or share your phone number for anyone else's marketing. We share it only with the services that run PRNTR for us: Whop (payments, memberships, Discord access), Render (hosting), Resend (email), Twilio (texts), Calendly (call booking), ManyChat and Instagram (DMs), and Discord (the community). We may share information if the law requires it or to answer a chargeback.

4. How long we keep it

As long as you're on our list or a student, and up to 7 years for payment and tax records. Applications that didn't go ahead are deleted after 2 years.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails with the link in any email; stop texts by replying STOP.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information through a DM on Instagram @instagram, a ticket in the PRNTR Discord, or Whop's Resolution Center. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK) you may have more rights, and we honor them.

6. Security and age

Your data is stored with the providers above using encrypted connections, and only PRNTR staff who need it can see it. PRNTR is for people 18 and older; we don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

7. Changes and contact

We'll post any change here with a new date. Questions: a DM on Instagram @instagram, a ticket in the PRNTR Discord, or Whop's Resolution Center.
