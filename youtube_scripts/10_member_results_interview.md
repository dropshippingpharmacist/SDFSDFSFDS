# Video: Member / student results interview

About 12–20 minutes. You can't script the guest's answers word for word; this is your exact script for the intro, every question and the close. Record a video call (Zoom, Riverside or Google Meet with recording on).

Before recording: get the member's written OK to publish, and verify their results (screenshots / exchange history). Never coach them to exaggerate.

---

**[FACE] INTRO (0:00–0:30)**

Today I've got [name] with me. [He/she/they] [one-line result, verified: e.g. "went from blowing accounts to [result] in [time]"]. I want you to hear it directly, the good and the bad.

[Name], welcome. Thanks for doing this.

**QUESTIONS (ask in this order; let them talk, don't interrupt)**

1. Before we met, where were you with trading? What was going wrong?
2. How long had you been trading, and roughly how much had you lost?
3. What made you decide to try the signals / the mentorship? Were you sceptical?
4. What was the first week like?
5. What changed? What do you do differently now?
6. Tell me about a trade that didn't work. How did you handle it?
7. Where are you now? [Ask for the specific, verified result.]
8. What's the biggest difference in your life outside trading?
9. What would you say to someone watching who's where you were before?
10. Anything you'd want improved? (Honest answers here build more trust than praise.)

**[FACE] CLOSE (last 30 seconds)**

[Name], thank you. Really proud of you.

If you want to get the same signals [name] uses, the link is below, and every trade is on the public results page.

Individual results vary and aren't typical. Not financial advice.
