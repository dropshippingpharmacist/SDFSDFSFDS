**[FACE] WHY THEY BLOW UP (0:15–3:00)**

Leverage doesn't make you a better trader. It makes your mistakes bigger, faster.

Here's how an account usually dies.

Step one: a trader uses high leverage and a big position, because the potential profit looks amazing.

Step two: the trade goes slightly against them. Normal market noise. But with that much leverage, slightly is enough to get them liquidated.

Step three: they're angry, so they open an even bigger trade to win it back.

Step four: repeat, until the account is gone.

Notice what's missing in that story. Nobody ever decided, before the trade, how much they were willing to lose.

**[CHART] RULE ONE: DECIDE THE LOSS FIRST (3:00–5:00)**

Before every trade, I know exactly how much money I lose if I'm wrong. In dollars.

Not "I'll see how it goes." A number.

[CHART: show an entry and a stop on a clean chart.]

The stop goes where the trade idea is clearly wrong. Then I size the position so that if price hits that stop, I lose a small, fixed amount of my account. [Your number, e.g. "one to two percent."]

Leverage just becomes a tool to reach that size. It's not a goal.

**[CHART] RULE TWO: ONLY TAKE TRADES THAT PAY MORE THAN THEY RISK (5:00–7:00)**

If my stop risks one dollar, I want the target to pay at least [two or three].

Why? Because then I can be wrong more often than I'm right and still make money.

Here's the math. Say you risk 1 to make 3. You take ten trades. You lose six of them. That's minus 6. You win four. That's plus 12. You're up 6, while losing most of your trades.

[Show it on screen as simple numbers.]

That's why my bot skips any setup where the target isn't far enough away.

**[CHART] RULE THREE: TAKE MOST OF THE PROFIT EARLY (7:00–8:30)**

When a trade reaches the first target, I take most of the position off. The rest runs toward the bigger target.

That means once a trade has worked a little, the pressure is off. Even if it comes back, most of the profit is already in the bank.

**[FACE] PUTTING IT TOGETHER (8:30–9:30)**

Decide the loss first. Only take trades that pay more than they risk. Take most of the profit early.

That's it. No secret indicator. Most people know these rules. Almost nobody follows them every single time.

That's exactly why I built a bot to follow them for me.
