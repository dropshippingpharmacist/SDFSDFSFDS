**[FACE] THE MISTAKE (0:15–1:45)**

Most people pick leverage first. "I'll use 20x." Then they pick a position size that feels exciting.

That's backwards. Leverage should be the last number you pick, not the first.

**[CHART / whiteboard] THE METHOD, STEP BY STEP (1:45–6:30)**

I'll use a simple example. These numbers are just for the example.

**Step one: your account.** Say you have ten thousand dollars.

**Step two: decide your risk per trade.** I risk a small fixed slice. In this example, one percent. One percent of ten thousand is one hundred dollars. That's the most this trade can lose.

**Step three: find your stop.** Look at the chart. Where is the trade idea clearly wrong? Say you buy at one hundred dollars and the stop is at ninety-eight. That's a two-dollar, or two percent, distance.

**Step four: work out the position size.** If price falls two percent and you want to lose one hundred dollars, your position must be five thousand dollars. Because two percent of five thousand is one hundred.

[Show on screen: position size = risk in dollars ÷ distance to stop.]

**Step five: now pick leverage.** You need a five-thousand-dollar position. You don't have to put five thousand in. With 5x leverage you'd use one thousand of margin. With 10x, five hundred.

Here's the key: the leverage does NOT change how much you lose at the stop. You still lose one hundred dollars. The leverage only changes how much margin is tied up.

**[CHART] THE ONE CHECK PEOPLE FORGET (6:30–7:45)**

Your liquidation price must be beyond your stop. Always.

If you use so much leverage that you'd be liquidated before price reaches your stop, your stop is fake. The exchange will close you out first, with fees on top.

My bot checks this on every single trade. If the liquidation price is too close, it lowers the leverage.

**[FACE] SUMMARY (7:45–8:30)**

Decide your risk in dollars. Find your stop on the chart. Size the position from those two. Then choose leverage, and make sure liquidation is beyond the stop.

Do that every time and no single trade can wipe you out.
